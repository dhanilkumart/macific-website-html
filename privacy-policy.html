Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Macific Holdings handles information in connection with its corporate website (macific.com) and outlines our data practices across our digital products and services.

1. Introduction

Macific Holdings ("we," "our," or "us") is a business based in Kozhikode, Kerala, India, specializing in digital products, online platforms, and web-based services. Our portfolio includes digital products and platforms such as GrekTheory, a product of Macific Holdings.

This Privacy Policy applies to our corporate website at https://macific.com/. This website is currently an informational corporate website designed to share information about our business activities, focus areas, and product offerings.

Where specific digital products, online platforms, or services operated by Macific Holdings (including GrekTheory) provide distinct functionality or maintain dedicated user terms, platform-specific privacy notices or terms of service will apply to those respective services.

2. Information We Collect

Because macific.com is an informational corporate website, we do not require visitors to create accounts or submit personal data to explore our content. Specifically:

  • No contact or enquiry forms: This website does not host online enquiry, feedback, or submission forms.
  • No user accounts: We do not offer user registration, profile creation, or account login functionality on this website.
  • No payment processing: We do not operate online checkouts, shopping carts, or payment gateway processing on this website.

Information is processed only in the following limited and transparent circumstances:

  • Direct Communications: If you choose to contact us directly via email at info@macific.com, we will receive the email address from which you send the message, along with any contact information, queries, or details you choose to share with us.
  • Automated Technical Information: When you browse this website, our web hosting provider and server infrastructure may automatically process standard network transmission data. Depending on server configuration and security logging, this technical data may conditionally include your IP address, browser type and version, operating system, requested page URL, access timestamp (date and time of visit), referring URL, and security diagnostic logs. We do not use this technical data to identify individual visitors personally.

3. How Information Is Used

Any information processed in relation to this website is used strictly for legitimate operational, administrative, and technical purposes, including:

  • Website operation: Delivering web pages reliably, securely, and correctly to your device.
  • Responding to inquiries: Reading, processing, and responding to direct inquiries or correspondence sent to our official email address.
  • Security and network integrity: Detecting, preventing, and mitigating malicious traffic, abuse, unauthorized access attempts, and system disruptions.
  • Technical diagnostics: Monitoring server health, analyzing technical errors, and maintaining service reliability and uptime.
  • Legal compliance: Fulfilling our legal or regulatory obligations where required under applicable Indian laws.

We do not sell, rent, or trade visitor data. We do not use information collected through this website for automated profiling, behavioral tracking, or commercial marketing.

4. Cookies and Similar Technologies

We respect your privacy and keep our website transparent:

  • We do not use advertising cookies or targeted marketing tags.
  • We do not use Meta Pixel.
  • We use Google Analytics (Google tag / gtag.js) to understand general website traffic and aggregate usage patterns. Google Analytics may set cookies on your browser to measure interactions on our site. These cookies do not store personally identifiable information. You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking through your browser settings or by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

Any other cookies or temporary session storage mechanisms that may be utilized are strictly limited to essential technical or security operations required by our web hosting infrastructure or content delivery network to serve pages safely.

You can configure your web browser to block or remove cookies at any time through your browser preferences. Since this website is purely informational, disabling cookies will not impair your ability to view and read our content.

5. Third-Party Services

To maintain, operate, and analyze this website, we may rely on trusted third-party infrastructure and service providers. These may include:

  • Web Analytics: Google Analytics (Google LLC) used to collect aggregated, anonymous website traffic statistics and performance metrics.
  • Web Hosting and Infrastructure: Server providers that store website assets, route network requests, and manage server security.
  • Typography & CDN: Font delivery services (such as Google Fonts) used to render web typography across modern browsers.
  • Email Service Providers: Secure mail infrastructure used to receive and manage email correspondence directed to info@macific.com.

Where such providers process technical data, they do so solely to the extent required to deliver the relevant technical service. We do not share visitor data with third-party advertising networks or data brokers.

6. Data Security

We apply reasonable administrative, physical, and technical safeguards designed to protect our infrastructure and any communications we receive against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or alteration. All traffic on macific.com is encrypted using HTTPS.

However, please note that no method of data transmission across the internet and no method of electronic storage can be guaranteed to be 100% secure. While we take reasonable precautions to safeguard our systems, we cannot warrant or guarantee absolute security.

7. Data Retention

Because macific.com does not maintain user accounts or collect form submissions, we do not store customer databases from website visits.

Standard server logs and security records processed by hosting infrastructure are retained only for temporary durations necessary for security monitoring, server health, and diagnostic analysis in accordance with standard hosting provider retention cycles.

If you email us directly, we retain your communication and email address only for as long as needed to address your query and satisfy legitimate operational, administrative, or legal record-keeping requirements.

8. Privacy Requests and User Rights

Under applicable Indian data protection laws and principles, you may have rights regarding personal information you communicate to us, including:

  • Requesting confirmation of whether we hold any personal information about you.
  • Requesting access to or correction of personal details submitted through correspondence.
  • Requesting the deletion of email communications you have previously sent to us, subject to applicable legal retention needs.

Because our website is informational and does not track individual visitors, we generally do not hold personal data unless you have contacted us by email. To submit a privacy request, please email us at info@macific.com. We will review and address verified requests within a reasonable timeframe.

9. Children's Privacy

Our website is designed for a general corporate audience and is not directed to or intended for children under the age of 18 (or the applicable age of majority).

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If a parent or guardian believes that a child has communicated personal information to us via email, please contact us at info@macific.com, and we will take prompt steps to remove and delete such information.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

Macific Holdings may update or revise this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our website functionality, operational practices, technologies, or applicable legal obligations.

When changes are made, the revised policy will be posted directly to this page with an updated "Last updated" date at the top. We encourage you to review this page periodically to stay informed of our ongoing privacy commitments.
